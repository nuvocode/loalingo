# Code of Conduct

Sprigo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Many people here are learning a language. Be patient with mistakes in English or any other language.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community, not only for yourself.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki) and when you represent the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **ozer@ozdas.org**. Every report is reviewed and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits and issues that don't follow this code. Depending on severity, a response ranges from a private correction or a warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
