# Getting help

- **Questions and setup** (installing, picking a model, microphone and voices): ask in [Q&A](https://github.com/ozerozdas/sprigo/discussions/categories/q-a-help).
- **Local models**: [AI & Local Models](https://github.com/ozerozdas/sprigo/discussions/categories/ai-local-models).
- **Bugs**: open a [bug report](https://github.com/ozerozdas/sprigo/issues/new?template=bug.yml) with your Sprigo version, OS and AI provider.
- **Ideas**: [Ideas & Feature Requests](https://github.com/ozerozdas/sprigo/discussions/categories/ideas-feature-requests).
- **Security issues**: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Please don't post them publicly.
